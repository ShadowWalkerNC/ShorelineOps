# CMS-2567 Survey Audit Binder

The `CmsSurveyEngine` (`server/src/engine/cmsSurvey.ts`) audits operations against federal Long-Term Care F-Tags:

- **F800**: Overall dietary service direction and qualified dietitian oversight.
- **F801**: Staffing sufficiency.
- **F802**: Qualified Dietary Director certification.
- **F803 / F804 / F805**: Palatable, nutritionally adequate food prepared to meet resident preferences.
- **F808**: Physician-ordered therapeutic diet conformity.
- **F809**: 14-hour maximum span between substantial evening meal and breakfast.
- **F812**: Food safety, sanitization, and 90-day HACCP holding temperature compliance.
