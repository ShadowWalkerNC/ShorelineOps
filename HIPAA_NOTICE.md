# Notice of Privacy Practices

**Shoreline Operations LLC**
**Effective Date:** July 8, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 8, 2026

---

> **THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT RESIDENTS MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.**

---

## 1. Who This Notice Covers

This Notice of Privacy Practices ("Notice") applies to Shoreline Operations LLC and all staff, contractors, and agents who access Protected Health Information (PHI) through the Shoreline Platform in the course of providing care and services.

---

## 2. Our Commitment to Privacy

We are required by law to:
- Maintain the privacy of your Protected Health Information
- Provide you with notice of our legal duties and privacy practices
- Follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect
- Notify affected individuals of a breach of unsecured PHI

---

## 3. What Is Protected Health Information?

Protected Health Information (PHI) is any individually identifiable information about a resident's:
- Past, present, or future physical or mental health condition
- Health care provided to the resident
- Past, present, or future payment for health care

PHI includes names, dates, dietary records, care notes, room assignments when linked to health information, and other identifying data connected to health information.

---

## 4. How We May Use and Disclose PHI

### 4.1 Treatment
We use and share PHI to coordinate and provide care services to residents, including dietary management, activity coordination, and care scheduling.

### 4.2 Health Care Operations
We use PHI for quality assurance, compliance, staff training, and operational management of the facility.

### 4.3 Required by Law
We will disclose PHI when required by federal, state, or local law, including in response to lawful court orders, subpoenas, or government investigations.

### 4.4 Public Health Activities
We may disclose PHI to public health authorities for activities authorized by law, such as reporting communicable diseases.

### 4.5 Serious Threats to Health or Safety
We may use or disclose PHI if necessary to prevent or lessen a serious and imminent threat to the health or safety of a person or the public.

### 4.6 Business Associates
We may share PHI with third-party service providers (Business Associates) who perform functions on our behalf, provided they have signed a Business Associate Agreement committing them to protecting PHI.

---

## 5. Uses and Disclosures Requiring Authorization

Except as described above, we will not use or disclose PHI without a written authorization. This includes:
- Marketing purposes
- Sale of PHI
- Psychotherapy notes (where applicable)
- Other uses not described in this Notice

Authorization may be revoked in writing at any time, except where we have already relied on it.

---

## 6. Resident Rights

Residents (and their authorized representatives) have the following rights with respect to their PHI:

### 6.1 Right of Access
Residents have the right to inspect and obtain copies of their PHI maintained by us, with limited exceptions. Requests should be submitted in writing.

### 6.2 Right to Amend
Residents may request correction of PHI they believe is inaccurate or incomplete. We may deny the request if the information was not created by us, is not part of our records, or is already accurate.

### 6.3 Right to an Accounting of Disclosures
Residents may request a list of certain disclosures of their PHI made by us during the prior six years, excluding disclosures for treatment, payment, health care operations, and certain other exceptions.

### 6.4 Right to Request Restrictions
Residents may request that we restrict how we use or disclose PHI. We are not required to agree to all restrictions, but will comply with agreed-upon restrictions unless disclosure is required by law or for emergency treatment.

### 6.5 Right to Confidential Communications
Residents may request that we communicate with them about PHI in a certain way or at a certain location.

### 6.6 Right to a Copy of This Notice
Residents may request a copy of this Notice at any time.

---

## 7. Minimum Necessary Standard

When using or disclosing PHI or requesting PHI from another entity, we make reasonable efforts to limit PHI to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose, consistent with 45 CFR § 164.514(d).

---

## 8. Staff Obligations

All staff who access PHI through the Platform are required to:
- Access only the PHI necessary for their specific assigned duties
- Maintain confidentiality of all PHI at all times
- Immediately report any suspected or confirmed breach of PHI
- Complete required HIPAA training as directed by Shoreline Operations LLC

---

## 9. Breach Notification

In the event of a breach of unsecured PHI, Shoreline Operations LLC will notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days following discovery of the breach, as required by the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule (45 CFR §§ 164.400–414).

Notice will include:
- Description of the breach and the PHI involved
- Steps individuals should take to protect themselves
- Steps we are taking to investigate, mitigate, and prevent recurrence
- Contact information for questions

---

## 10. Changes to This Notice

We reserve the right to change the terms of this Notice. Changes will apply to all PHI we maintain. Updated Notices will be posted in the Platform and made available upon request. We will not apply a change retroactively without providing advance notice.
