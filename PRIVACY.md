**Last Updated:** July 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Shoreline Operations LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Shoreline staff management platform (the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when authorized staff members access the Platform.

This Platform is an **internal, staff-only tool**. It is not open to the general public, residents, or their families. Access is strictly limited to authorized employees and contractors of Shoreline Operations LLC.

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and agents who access the Platform
- All data processed through the Platform in connection with facility operations
- All devices and networks used to access the Platform

This Policy **does not** apply to:
- Personal devices or applications not authorized by Shoreline Operations LLC
- Third-party websites or services that may be linked from the Platform

---

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Staff Account Information
- Full name, job title, and role/permission level
- Work email address
- Assigned facility or department
- Account credentials (passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext)
- Date of account creation and last login

### 3.2 Activity and Audit Logs
- Actions performed within the Platform (record creation, edits, deletions)
- Timestamps and IP addresses associated with Platform access
- Session duration and feature usage

### 3.3 Resident-Related Data Entered by Staff
- Resident names, room assignments, and care-related notes
- Dietary restrictions, preferences, and meal records
- Activity participation and scheduling data
- Any other Protected Health Information (PHI) entered in the course of care coordination

### 3.4 Technical Data
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Device identifiers
- Error logs and diagnostic data

---

## 4. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely for legitimate operational purposes:

- **Care coordination:** Enabling staff to record, access, and act on resident care information
- **Facility operations:** Supporting scheduling, dietary management, and activity planning
- **Security and compliance:** Monitoring for unauthorized access, policy violations, and HIPAA compliance
- **Audit trails:** Maintaining records of data access and modification as required by law
- **Platform improvement:** Diagnosing technical issues and improving system performance
- **Legal obligations:** Responding to lawful requests from regulatory bodies

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade any staff or resident data to third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 5. HIPAA Compliance

The Platform processes Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and its implementing regulations (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164).

Shoreline Operations LLC operates as a **Covered Entity** or **Business Associate** as applicable and maintains:

- A current Notice of Privacy Practices (see `HIPAA_NOTICE.md`)
- Business Associate Agreements with all applicable third-party vendors
- Technical, administrative, and physical safeguards as required by the HIPAA Security Rule
- Breach notification procedures as required by the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule

All staff who access PHI through the Platform are subject to HIPAA training requirements and confidentiality obligations.

Retention periods may be extended where required by applicable law, litigation hold, or regulatory investigation.

## 8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.2 or higher for all data transmissions
- **Encryption at rest:** AES-256 encryption for all stored data
- **Access controls:** Role-based permissions ensuring staff access only data necessary for their job function
- **Authentication:** Secure login with session management
- **Infrastructure:** Hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL) and Render, both of which maintain SOC 2 certifications
- **Audit logging:** All data access and modification events are logged
- **Vulnerability management:** Regular dependency auditing and security patching

## 11. Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach involving PHI or personal data, Shoreline Operations LLC will:

1. Investigate and contain the breach promptly
2. Notify affected individuals in accordance with HIPAA's Breach Notification Rule (within 60 days of discovery, or sooner as required)
3. Notify the Secretary of Health and Human Services as required
4. Notify state regulators as required by applicable state breach notification laws
5. Document the incident and remediation steps
