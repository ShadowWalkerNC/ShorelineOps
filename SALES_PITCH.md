## Executive Summary

**ShorelineOps** is the open-source, HIPAA-ready dietary operations and clinical care coordination platform designed specifically for **Assisted Living, Memory Care, and Skilled Nursing Facilities**.

It eliminates vendor lock-in, reduces food and labor spend by **$1.50–$3.00 per resident day**, prevents life-threatening allergen errors, and bridges kitchen production with food distributor order guides (featuring a reference adapter for **Dennis Food Service**).

---

## 🍽️ The Problem in Senior Living Foodservice

1. **High Operating Costs & Food Waste**:
   - Foodservice is typically the 2nd largest operating cost in senior living after direct care nursing.
   - Without dynamic par tracking, facilities routinely over-order perishable inventory or face emergency out-of-stock runs.
2. **Clinical Safety & State Citations**:
   - Dietary and texture errors (e.g. serving regular texture to a pureed/dysphagia resident) are leading causes of state survey deficiencies and resident hospitalizations.
3. **Distributor Inflexibility**:
   - Legacy enterprise software locks kitchens into single-distributor proprietary systems with opaque markups and expensive per-bed licensing fees ($1,200+/month).

1. **$1.50–$3.00 / Resident Day Savings**:

2. **Zero-Error Tray Line Execution**:
   - **Kitchen Tablet Mode (`/kitchen/tablet`)** provides cooks with a high-contrast touch interface displaying bold red allergen warnings, texture requirements, and beverage orders with 1-tap cart verification.

   - Distributor sales reps have dedicated portal access (`/distributor`) to update contract unit pricing without accessing resident medical data.
