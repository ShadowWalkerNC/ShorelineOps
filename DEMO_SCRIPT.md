# ShorelineOps — Guided 5-Minute Live Sales Demo Script

**Audience:** Executive Director, Director of Nursing (DON), and Dietary Manager  
**Duration:** 5 to 7 Minutes  
**Demo URL:** `http://localhost:3000` (or your live staging domain)  
**Login:** `admin@shoreline.demo` / `Admin1234!`

---

## 🎬 Act 1: The Resident Safety Story (Minute 0:00 - 1:30)

1. **Open Dashboard & Navigate to Residents**:
   - Click **`Residents & Diets`** in the left sidebar.
   - *Presenter Script*:  
     > *"In senior living, dietary safety is where kitchens face the highest clinical risk. Let’s look at Eleanor Vance or Arthur Pendelton."*
2. **Show Clinical Profile Details**:
   - Click into a resident profile. Highlight the **Therapeutic Diet** (e.g. Pureed, NAS, Renal), **IDDSI Texture**, and **Allergies** (e.g. Shellfish, Peanuts).
   - *Presenter Script*:  
     > *"Notice how the system tracks texture modifications, required thickeners, and critical allergen exclusions. When nursing or the dietitian updates this profile, it instantly updates the entire kitchen in real time."*

---

## 🍳 Act 2: Kitchen Tablet Mode & Tray Cards (Minute 1:30 - 3:00)

1. **Switch to Kitchen Tablet Mode**:
   - Click **`Kitchen Tablet Mode`** in the sidebar (`/kitchen/tablet`).
   - *Presenter Script*:  
     > *"Now let’s look at what your cooks see on their kitchen iPad or wall-mounted tablet during prep and service."*
2. **Demo the 3 Tablet Views**:
   - **Cook Worksheets**: Tap to scale batch portions and toggle status (`pending` ➔ `prepping` ➔ `completed`).
   - **Tray Card Dispatch**: Click the **Tray Card Line** tab. Highlight the high-contrast card with the **BOLD RED ALLERGEN WARNING**. Tap **"✅ VERIFY & DISPATCH TRAY TO CART"**.
   - **Quick Par Counter**: Tap `+` and `-` to show how cooks adjust physical on-hand case counts during a 2-minute morning walk-through.
   - *Presenter Script*:  
     > *"Cooks don’t have to type or scroll through complex software. They get big, touch-friendly buttons that make it impossible to serve the wrong meal to an allergic or dysphagia resident."*

---

## 🛒 Act 3: Dennis Food Service Auto-Ordering (Minute 3:00 - 4:30)

1. **Navigate to Purchasing & Orders**:
   - Click **`Purchasing & Orders`** (`/purchasing`).
2. **Demo Suggested Order Generator**:
   - Click the **Suggested Order Generator** tab.
   - *Presenter Script*:  
     > *"Here is how Shoreline saves your facility $1.50 to $3.00 per resident day. The system looks at your standing order guide, compares your par levels with what was counted on the tablet, and automatically builds your suggested purchase order."*
3. **Show Dennis CSV Export & Drag-and-Drop Import**:
   - Click **"📥 Export Dennis CSV Order"** to show the instant order file ready for Dennis Food Service.
   - Show the **"📥 Import Dennis CSV Guide"** modal demonstrating how fast new distributor item masters can be dropped in.

---

## 📊 Act 4: Cost & State Compliance Audit (Minute 4:30 - 5:30)

1. **Navigate to Cost & Compliance Reports**:
   - Click **`Cost & Compliance`** (`/reporting`).
2. **Highlight Key Financial & Inspection Metrics**:
   - Point out **Food Cost / Resident Day ($10.86/day)** and **Total Operating / Res Day ($18.42/day)** incorporating dietary labor.
   - Point out **Allergy Audit Flags** and **Substitutions Log**.
3. **Click "🖨️ Print Compliance Summary"**:
   - *Presenter Script*:  
     > *"When the State Department of Health walks in for an unannounced annual survey, you don't spend hours digging through paper binders. In one click, you hand the surveyor a complete, audited Dietary Compliance Summary."*

---

## 🤝 The Close (Minute 5:30)

> *"Shoreline takes less than 10 minutes to initialize with your wings and dining rooms. We offer a 30-day risk-free pilot where we pre-load your census and Dennis order guide. Would you like us to set up your facility's pilot instance this week?"*
