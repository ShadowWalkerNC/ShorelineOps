## 📢 3. Go-To-Market Execution (How to Market & Close Facilities)

### Channel 1: The Dennis Food Service Channel Multiplier (Warmest Route)
- **Why it works**: Dennis Food Service sales reps already visit every dietary manager and executive director in your region every week.
- **The Pitch to Dennis Reps**:
  > *"When your accounts guess inventory on clipboards, they under-order or experience shortages. ShorelineOps gives them an automated par generator that maintains consistent weekly order volume with Dennis. We give you free access to the `/distributor` portal to manage item pricing directly."*
- **Action**: Schedule a 20-minute meeting with your local Dennis territory sales manager to present the Dennis integration.

### Channel 2: The "Survey Season Panic" Campaign
- **Why it works**: Senior living facilities scramble when state health inspection survey windows open. A citation for a dietary texture error or lack of substitution documentation can cost thousands in fines.
- **The Hook**: *"Is your kitchen ready for an unannounced state survey tomorrow? Shoreline gives you a 1-click Printable Dietary Compliance Summary and auditable substitution log."*
- **Target**: Directors of Nursing (DONs) and Dietary Directors via LinkedIn and state LTC newsletters (e.g., Maine Health Care Association).

### Channel 3: ANFP & Local CDM Chapter Networking
- **Why it works**: The **Association of Nutrition & Foodservice Professionals (ANFP)** is where Certified Dietary Managers (CDMs) gather.
- **Action**: Attend local Maine/regional ANFP chapter meetings or sponsor a lunch. Offer a free 30-day pilot to 3 local facilities in exchange for case study quotes and data.

### Channel 4: The 30-Day "Risk-Free Pilot" Offer
- Don't ask for a 12-month contract upfront.
- **The Irresistible Offer**:
  1. We import their resident census and Dennis order guide in 10 minutes.
  2. We set up an iPad in their kitchen for 30 days free.
  3. If they don't save at least 5 hours on ordering and eliminate tray card errors, they pay nothing.

## 💰 4. Sales Battlecard: Direct Competitor Objection Handling

### When the Prospect Says: *"We're looking at MealSuite."*
> **Your Response**: *"MealSuite is great if you're a 500-bed hospital chain with a dedicated IT team and a $15,000 annual software budget. But for a 60-bed independent facility, you're paying for 80% of features you'll never use. Shoreline was built inside a working kitchen—our Kitchen Tablet Mode takes 5 minutes to learn, works directly with Dennis Food Service, and costs $399/month with no multi-year lock-in."*

### When the Prospect Says: *"We just use binders and clipboards—it's free."*
> **Your Response**: *"Binders are free until a state surveyor cites you for an undocumented pureed substitution, or an agency cook plates a meal with peanuts for an allergic resident. Shoreline pays for itself in the first week by stopping over-ordering on perishable produce and giving your cooks big red allergy alerts on the tray line."*

### When the Prospect Says: *"We need our food distributor to support it."*
> **Your Response**: *"Unlike legacy systems that lock you into one proprietary vendor, Shoreline is distributor-agnostic. We already have direct support for Dennis Food Service, Sysco, and US Foods via simple CSV guide sync. Your rep can even update prices directly in the portal."*
