# ShorelineOps Master Services & Software License Agreement (SaaS / On-Premise)

**Effective Date:** ________________________  
**Client / Facility Name:** ____________________________________________ ("Customer")  
**Provider:** Shoreline Operations Platform ("Provider")  

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") governs Customer's access to and use of the ShorelineOps dietary management and operations software.

---

### 1. License & Subscription Scope
1.1 **Grant of License**: Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, subscription-based license to access and utilize the ShorelineOps software for internal care coordination, dietary management, and foodservice operations at the facility location(s) specified in the Order Form.  
1.2 **User Tiers**: Customer may provision user accounts conforming to the 10 defined Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) tiers (Super Admin, Dietitian, Dietary Manager, Kitchen Staff, Distributor Partner, Server, etc.).

---

### 2. Fees, Billing & Term
2.1 **Subscription Fees**: Customer agrees to pay the monthly or annual subscription fee associated with their selected tier (Starter $199/mo, Professional $399/mo, or Enterprise $699/mo) as set forth in the applicable Order Form.  
2.2 **Payment Terms**: Fees are billed in advance on a recurring monthly or annual cycle. Invoices are due net-30 days from date of issuance.  
2.3 **Term & Termination**: Either party may terminate this Agreement without penalty by providing thirty (30) days written notice prior to the expiration of the current billing period. Upon termination, Customer retains full ownership and export rights to all resident records and purchasing history.

---

### 3. Healthcare Data Privacy & HIPAA Compliance
3.1 **Business Associate Status**: Provider acknowledges its status as a "Business Associate" under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA") and the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act ("HITECH").  
3.2 **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)**: The terms of Provider's standard BAA (appended hereto as Exhibit A and executed via `/setup`) are incorporated into this Agreement by reference.  
3.3 **Security Safeguards**: Provider maintains administrative, technical, and physical safeguards including append-only immutable audit logs, session auto-logouts, encryption in transit (TLS 1.3), and encryption at rest (AES-256).

---

### 4. Distributor Agnosticism & Data Ownership
4.1 **Customer Data Ownership**: Customer retains 100% sole and exclusive ownership of all resident Protected Health Information (PHI), menu cycles, recipe books, inventory logs, and purchasing records.  
4.2 **Distributor Freedom**: ShorelineOps is distributor-agnostic. Provider shall not restrict, alter, or monetize Customer's choice of primary broadline food distributor (e.g., Dennis Food Service, Sysco, US Foods, Gordon Food Service).

---

### 5. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Support
5.1 **System Availability**: Provider targets 99.9% application uptime, excluding scheduled off-hours maintenance windows.  
5.2 **Technical Support**: Professional and Enterprise subscribers receive priority email and portal support with standard response times under 4 business hours.
