## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meaning given to them in the HIPAA Rules (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164).

**1.1 "Breach"** means the acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of PHI in a manner not permitted under the Privacy Rule that compromises the security or privacy of the PHI, as defined at 45 CFR § 164.402.

**1.2 "Business Associate"** has the meaning given at 45 CFR § 160.103.

**1.3 "Covered Entity"** means Shoreline Operations LLC.

**1.4 "Designated Record Set"** has the meaning given at 45 CFR § 164.501.

**1.5 "Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI)"** has the meaning given at 45 CFR § 160.103, limited to ePHI created, received, maintained, or transmitted by Business Associate from or on behalf of Covered Entity.

**1.6 "HIPAA Rules"** means collectively the Privacy Rule, Security Rule, Breach Notification Rule, and Enforcement Rule promulgated by HHS.

**1.7 "Protected Health Information (PHI)"** has the meaning given at 45 CFR § 160.103, limited to PHI created, received, maintained, or transmitted by Business Associate from or on behalf of Covered Entity.

**1.8 "Subcontractor"** has the meaning given at 45 CFR § 160.103.

## ARTICLE 2 — OBLIGATIONS OF BUSINESS ASSOCIATE

**2.1 Permitted Uses and Disclosures.** Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only:
- As necessary to perform the services described in the Services Agreement
- As required by law
- As otherwise permitted in writing by Covered Entity
- For Business Associate's proper management and administration, provided: (a) the disclosure is required by law, or (b) Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurances from the recipient regarding confidentiality and breach notification

**2.2 Prohibited Uses and Disclosures.** Business Associate shall not:
- Use or disclose PHI other than as permitted by this Agreement or the HIPAA Rules
- Use PHI for Business Associate's own purposes, including marketing or product development
- Sell PHI, as defined at 45 CFR § 164.502(a)(5)(ii)
- De-identify PHI and use resulting de-identified data in ways that could re-identify individuals

**2.3 Safeguards.** Business Associate shall implement and maintain appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect PHI and ePHI, including all measures required by the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR §§ 164.308, 164.310, 164.312).

**2.4 Minimum Necessary.** Business Associate shall make reasonable efforts to use, disclose, and request only the minimum necessary PHI to accomplish the intended purpose.

**2.5 Subcontractors.** Business Associate shall ensure that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees to the same restrictions and conditions as apply to Business Associate under this Agreement, by entering into a written agreement with the Subcontractor that complies with 45 CFR § 164.504(e)(2).

**2.6 Reporting.**

(a) *Security Incidents.* Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any Security Incident (as defined at 45 CFR § 164.304) of which it becomes aware without unreasonable delay and in no event later than **[SECURITY_INCIDENT_NOTICE_DAYS, e.g., 10]** business days after Business Associate's discovery.

(b) *Breaches.* Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity without unreasonable delay and in no event later than **[BREACH_NOTICE_DAYS, e.g., 3]** business days after discovery of a Breach of Unsecured PHI, as required by 45 CFR § 164.410. Notice shall include the information required by 45 CFR § 164.410(c).

(c) *Non-Permitted Disclosures.* Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement of which it becomes aware.

**2.7 Individual Rights Assistance.** Business Associate shall cooperate with Covered Entity to facilitate the exercise of Individual rights, including:
- Providing access to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed by Covered Entity
- Amending PHI in a Designated Record Set at Covered Entity's direction
- Providing an accounting of disclosures as requested

**2.8 Access by HHS.** Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary of HHS for purposes of determining Covered Entity's or Business Associate's compliance with HIPAA Rules, subject to applicable legal privileges.

**2.9 Return or Destruction of PHI.** Upon termination of the Services Agreement, Business Associate shall, at Covered Entity's election, return or destroy all PHI received from or created on behalf of Covered Entity. If return or destruction is infeasible, Business Associate shall extend the protections of this Agreement to retained PHI and limit further use to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, as required by 45 CFR § 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(J).

## ARTICLE 3 — OBLIGATIONS OF COVERED ENTITY

**3.1 Permissible Requests.** Covered Entity shall not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under the Privacy Rule.

**3.2 Notice of Restrictions.** Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI that Covered Entity has agreed to or is required to abide by under 45 CFR § 164.522.

**3.3 Authorizations.** Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any changes in, or revocation of, permission by an Individual to use or disclose PHI.

**3.4 Notice of Privacy Practices.** Covered Entity shall provide Business Associate with any changes in its Notice of Privacy Practices that affect Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — TERM AND TERMINATION

**4.1 Term.** This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues until termination of the Services Agreement, unless terminated earlier pursuant to this Article.

**4.2 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other Party has materially breached this Agreement and failed to cure such breach within **30 days** of receiving written notice of the breach.

**4.3 Immediate Termination by Covered Entity.** Covered Entity may immediately terminate this Agreement upon written notice if Covered Entity determines that Business Associate has committed a pattern or practice that constitutes a material violation of this Agreement and cure is not feasible.

**4.4 Effect of Termination.** Sections 2.9 (Return or Destruction of PHI), Article 5 (General Provisions), and all confidentiality and data protection obligations shall survive termination.

## ARTICLE 5 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**5.1 Regulatory Compliance.** The Parties acknowledge that HIPAA Rules are subject to change and agree that this Agreement shall be automatically amended to the extent necessary to comply with applicable changes in HIPAA Rules.

**5.4 Amendment.** This Agreement may not be amended except by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

**5.5 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Services Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement of the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

**5.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by federal law and, where applicable, the laws of the State of **[GOVERNING_STATE_PLACEHOLDER]**.

**5.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**5.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, including electronic counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
