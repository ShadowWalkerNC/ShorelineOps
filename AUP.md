# Acceptable Use Policy

**Shoreline Operations LLC**
**Effective Date:** July 8, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 8, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") establishes rules for the responsible, ethical, and lawful use of the Shoreline staff management platform (the "Platform") and any related systems, networks, and data. This Policy supplements and incorporates by reference the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

All authorized users — employees, contractors, and agents of Shoreline Operations LLC — must comply with this AUP as a condition of Platform access.

---

## 2. Core Principles

All Platform use must be:
- **Lawful** — in compliance with HIPAA, applicable state law, and federal law
- **Purposeful** — limited to legitimate job duties
- **Minimal** — accessing only the data necessary for the specific task at hand (minimum necessary standard)
- **Respectful** — treating all resident and colleague data with dignity and professionalism
- **Secure** — taking reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure

---

## 3. Resident Data — Special Obligations

Resident data — including medical information, dietary records, care notes, and activity data — is Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA. Staff must:

- Access PHI only when directly required by their assigned duties
- Never discuss, transmit, or share PHI outside of authorized Platform workflows
- Never copy PHI to personal devices, personal email, text messages, or unauthorized cloud storage
- Never take screenshots or photos of screens displaying PHI
- Immediately report any accidental disclosure, suspected breach, or loss of PHI to their supervisor and to **[LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER]**

Violating PHI protections may result in personal civil and criminal liability under HIPAA, in addition to Company disciplinary action.

---

## 4. Account and Credential Use

- Each staff member is issued a unique account. Accounts are non-transferable.
- Passwords must not be written down, shared, or stored in an unsecured manner.
- Accounts must be logged out when unattended, even briefly.
- Staff who observe others using their account must report this immediately.
- Accounts should not be accessed from unsecured public Wi-Fi without VPN or equivalent security measures.

---

## 5. Device and Access Environment

- The Platform should be accessed on **Company-managed or authorized devices** where possible.
- If accessed on a personal device, the device must:
  - Be password-protected
  - Have up-to-date security software
  - Not be shared with household members during active Platform sessions
- Unattended devices with active Platform sessions must be locked immediately.

---

## 6. Data Entry Standards

Staff entering data into the Platform must:
- Enter accurate, complete, and timely information
- Not fabricate, falsify, or omit material care information
- Correct errors promptly and document corrections appropriately
- Avoid entering information outside their authorized scope (e.g., medical diagnoses by non-clinical staff)

Falsification of resident records is a serious violation and may result in immediate termination and regulatory reporting.

---

## 7. Prohibited Activities

The following are expressly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, as well as potential civil or criminal liability:

| Category | Prohibited Action |
|----------|------------------|
| **Data access** | Accessing resident records not related to your assigned duties |
| **Data export** | Exporting, printing, or transmitting PHI to unauthorized parties |
| **Credential abuse** | Sharing passwords or using another user's credentials |
| **System tampering** | Attempting to bypass access controls, audit logging, or security features |
| **Unauthorized software** | Installing browser extensions, scripts, or tools that interact with the Platform without authorization |
| **Personal use** | Using the Platform for any purpose unrelated to Shoreline Operations LLC work duties |
| **Harassment** | Accessing or using resident or colleague data in a harassing, discriminatory, or retaliatory manner |
| **Disclosure** | Discussing PHI or confidential business data in public spaces, personal social media, or unauthorized channels |
| **Recording** | Taking photos, videos, or screenshots of Platform screens displaying PHI |

---

## 8. Incident Reporting

All staff are **required** (not merely encouraged) to promptly report:
- Suspected or confirmed unauthorized access to the Platform or data
- Loss or theft of a device used to access the Platform
- Accidental disclosure of PHI to an unauthorized party
- Any observed violation of this AUP by a colleague

Reports should be made to an immediate supervisor and to **[LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER]**.

Retaliation against staff who report good-faith AUP violations or privacy concerns is strictly prohibited.

---

## 9. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this AUP may result in:
- Immediate suspension or permanent revocation of Platform access
- Formal disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract
- Civil liability under HIPAA or applicable state law
- Criminal prosecution where warranted
- Reporting to relevant licensing boards or regulatory agencies

Shoreline Operations LLC reserves the right to investigate all suspected violations and to use audit log data, access records, and other evidence in such investigations.
