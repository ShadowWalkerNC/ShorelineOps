# ShorelineOps Brand Identity & Logo System

> **Concept:** *The Pureed Horizon & Caduceus*  
> **Brand Purpose:** Open-source healthcare dietary operations, clinical nutrition, and care coordination platform. Engineered by a healthcare executive chef for senior living (Assisted Living, Memory Care, SNF, CCRC, Acute Care). IDDSI dysphagia safety, resident therapeutic diets, touch tablet batch cookery, split MRP purchasing, and CMS-2567 survey readiness.

---

## 1. Visual Metaphor & Symbolic Logic

1. **The Pureed Horizon Curve:**
   - Soft, flowing coastal wave arc representing the tranquil Maine shoreline and compassionate resident sanctuary.
   - Mathematically models the smooth rheological flow required for **IDDSI Level 4 Pureed** and modified-texture diets.
2. **The Inverted IDDSI Triangle & Level 4 Bar:**
   - The inverted equilateral triangle mirrors the internationally recognized IDDSI clinical framework (Levels 0–7).
   - The central horizontal cross-bar demarcates IDDSI Level 4 (the critical dividing line between liquids and modified solids).
3. **The Caduceus Staff & Care Wings:**
   - The ascending central beam and dual harmonic curves fuse clinical healthcare oversight with culinary execution.
   - Denotes the tight loop between the Registered Dietitian (RD), Executive Chef, and Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP).
4. **The Outer Clinical Shield:**
   - Rounded rectangle container providing clinical protection, HIPAA privacy, allergy collision defense, and survey readiness.
5. **Accreditation Crown Spark:**
   - The apex node atop the caduceus beam symbolizes CMS-2567 federal survey readiness and immaculate state inspection binders.

## 4. Usage Rules & Clear Space

- **Minimum Clear Space:** Maintain padding equal to the height of the caduceus apex node around all perimeter edges.
- **Minimum Size:**
  - Digital Icon: `24×24px`
  - Horizontal Lockup: `130×30px`
- **Integrity Rule:**
  - Never decouple the horizon wave from the IDDSI matrix in clinical contexts where diet orders or medication passes are displayed.
